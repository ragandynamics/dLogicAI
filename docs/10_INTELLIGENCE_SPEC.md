# dLogicAI Conversation Intelligence Specification
Target intelligence includes:
- intent
- sentiment/emotion
- frustration/urgency
- customer effort/confusion
- purchase/upsell/cross-sell signals
- churn/escalation/refund risk
- conversion/abandonment probability
- entities/topics/preferences/constraints
- next-best action/offer

Intelligence processing may be synchronous for lightweight signals and asynchronous for deeper analysis.
