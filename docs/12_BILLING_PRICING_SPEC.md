# dLogicAI Billing & Pricing Specification
Pricing must be configurable.

## Commercial model
- Free tier.
- Subscription plans.
- Bundled offers.
- Add-ons.
- Overage billing.
- Connector API-call allowances and billable overages.
- Cost estimator.
- Optional Stripe integration.

## Rule
Customer price must be calculated from configurable pricing rules, not hard-coded provider-cost multipliers.
